L. Garrett Anglin

Attorney

garrett@anglinlaw.net

Garrett graduated with College Honors and Departmental Honors from UCLA in 2014, where he concentrated his studies in Linguistics and Philosophy. He earned his Juris Doctor from Tulane University Law School in New Orleans, Louisiana, where he built a strong foundation in trial advocacy through the Litigation Clinic and as a competitor on the Interschool Trial Moot Court team.

Since joining the firm, Garrett has litigated numerous trials and focuses his practice on civil litigation, business disputes, and contract matters. His analytical background combined with hands-on courtroom experience enables him to develop effective litigation strategies and achieve favorable results for his clients.

In addition to his litigation practice, Garrett handles a wide range of real estate matters, including residential and commercial closings, complex property transactions, and disputes arising from contracts for the sale of land. Whether in the courtroom or at the closing table, Garrett takes pride in guiding clients through significant legal matters and fiercely protecting their interests.

L. Gilbert Anglin

Attorney

gilbert@anglinlaw.net

Gilbert graduated from Austin Peay State University in 1977 and earned his Juris Doctor from what is now the University of Memphis School of Law in 1982. Shortly after graduation, he moved to Murfreesboro, Tennessee, where he has been engaged in the private practice of law for over four decades.

Though his office is in Murfreesboro, Gilbert has represented clients throughout the State of Tennessee. He has tried cases before juries in numerous counties, including Davidson, Shelby, Montgomery, and Hamilton Counties, in both federal and state courts.

Gilbert's extensive trial experience includes personal injury cases involving automobile accidents, healthcare liability, premises liability, and workers' compensation. He has recovered awards for his clients, through settlement and trial, in excess of one million dollars. He also represents small business owners and individuals in business litigation and contract disputes.

In addition to his trial work, Gilbert maintains an active appellate practice. He has successfully argued cases before the Tennessee Court of Appeals, the Tennessee Supreme Court, and the Sixth Circuit Court of Appeals.

Gilbert does not represent insurance companies. His career has been dedicated to helping individuals and families navigate life's most challenging legal matters. If you need help, give him a call.
