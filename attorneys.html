L. Garrett Anglin

Attorney at Law

garrett@anglinlaw.net 615-895-8880

Garrett graduated with College Honors and Departmental Honors from UCLA in 2014, where he concentrated his studies in Linguistics and Philosophy. He earned his Juris Doctor from Tulane University Law School in New Orleans, Louisiana, where he developed a strong foundation in trial advocacy through the Litigation Clinic and as a competitor on the Interschool Trial Moot Court team.

Garrett focuses his practice on civil litigation, with extensive experience in constitutional rights matters and contract disputes. His analytical background and courtroom training enable him to build compelling cases and advocate effectively for his clients.

Working alongside his father, Garrett also handles real estate matters, including residential and commercial closings, property transactions, and disputes arising from contracts for the sale of land.
