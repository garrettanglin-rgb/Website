About Our Firm

Serving Middle Tennessee with integrity and dedication

Our Practice

Anglin Law was established with a simple mission: to provide exceptional legal representation to the people and businesses of Middle Tennessee. Located in Murfreesboro at Lincoln Square, we've had the privilege of serving our community through times of growth and change.

As Rutherford County has grown, so has our firm's experience and expertise. We've handled matters ranging from straightforward real estate closings to complex business litigation, always maintaining our commitment to personalized attention and responsive service.

Today, we continue to serve clients with the same values that founded our practice: honesty, hard work, and a genuine commitment to achieving the best possible outcomes for those we represent.
